# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Telehealth & Clinic Scheduling

Purpose
Establish a consistent, patient-centered workflow for booking, confirming, rescheduling, and following up on appointments while minimizing no-shows and scheduling conflicts.

Scope
Applies to virtual consultations, remote patient monitoring (RPM) onboarding/check-ins, and follow-up visits managed for healthcare providers and care teams.

1. Booking New Appointments
- Verify Patient Details: Confirm full name, Date of Birth, preferred contact method, and current physical location/time zone.

- Check Time Zones Explicitly: Always state both the patient's local time zone and the provider/clinic time zone when confirming a slot to prevent confusion.

- Match Visit Type & Duration: Allocate the right slot length (e.g., new intake vs. brief follow-up) and check provider availability rules or buffer blocks.

- Send Pre-Visit Prep: Share intake forms, consent links, or telehealth connection instructions with clear completion deadlines.

2. Appointment Reminders & Confirmations
- 48–72 Hours Before: Send an initial reminder with date, time (with time zone), provider name, and reschedule instructions.
- 24 Hours Before: Send a final confirmation prompt and verify intake forms or lab/insurance items are completed.
- Day-of Check: Flag unconfirmed slots or incomplete pre-visit items early so the provider and care team have clear visibility.

3. Rescheduling, Cancellations & No-Shows
- Reschedule / Cancellation: Offer alternative times promptly, note the reason in the schedule/chart per clinic policy, and open the freed slot if appropriate.
- No-Show Outreach (Within 10–15 Minutes): Attempt outreach via the patient's preferred contact channel, document the attempt clearly, and follow provider/clinic instructions for rebooking or clinical escalation if needed.

4. Waitlist & Schedule Optimization
- Fill Cancellations: Maintain a prioritized list of patients requesting earlier slots and reach out quickly when openings appear.
- Protect Focus Blocks: Respect provider charting, huddle, or urgent-slot blocks unless exceptions are approved.
