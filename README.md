Medical Virtual Assistant & Telehealth Portfolio

Welcome! I am a United States (New Mexico Board of Nursing) Registered Nurse and experienced medical virtual assistant , Telehealth & Healthcare Operations Specialist. This repository showcases standardized workflows, administrative templates, and patient care coordination resources designed for remote healthcare support and medical virtual assistance.

Core Competencies
- Telehealth & Virtual Triage:
Structured patient symptom intake, clinical call scripts, escalation protocols, and remote patient monitoring (RPM) tracking.

- Clinical & EHR Documentation: Standardized SOAP note templates, accurate charting workflows, referral coordination checklists, and follow-up summaries.

- ⁠Patient Intake & Scheduling: 
Multi-timezone appointment management, patient onboarding forms, insurance verification checklists, and automated reminder templates.


- Privacy & Compliance: 
Strict adherence to HIPAA-compliant communication standards, patient confidentiality, and secure data handling.
